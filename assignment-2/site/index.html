How it works, and how to check it

The method
  • Byte-fallback BPE (SentencePiece/Llama style). Subword BPE over character atoms; any character the tokenizer never saw falls back to its raw UTF-8 bytes. So it is efficient for Indic scripts and faithful: decode(encode(text)) keeps every visible character, for any input.
  • No lossy normalizer. NFKC would re-spell characters (″→′′); we omit it so the round trip is exact.
  • Decoupled per-language weighting. English, Hindi, Telugu and Marathi are weighted during training to balance their tokens-per-unit ratios, minimizing the spread within one shared 10k vocabulary.
  • Faithful corpus, not clipped prose. Each India article is fetched from Wikipedia and converted to text that keeps links, tables, references and punctuation, so the ratios reflect real article content and the round trip is tested on exactly what is trained.
Why the numbers are trustworthy
  • The widget tokenizes the shipped texts live and re-checks the round trip in your browser. Nothing is hardcoded.
  • The JavaScript tokenizer is verified token-for-token against the Python tokenizers library.
  • A standalone verify.py reproduces every figure and asserts decode(encode(x)) == x, using the assignment's own score formula so the numbers are independently checkable.
